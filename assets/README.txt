Image Format and Quality:
------------------------
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Quality: High (save at 80-90% quality to reduce file size)
- Resolution: Keep original resolution, allow responsive scaling in CSS
- Style: Dark theme with warm tones, authentic Japanese food photography

Lighting and Composition:
-----------------------
- Use natural lighting or professional studio lighting
- Dark, moody background works well
- Focus on quality and texture of food
- Include minimal props that complement the food
- Maintain consistent styling across all images
- Show the chef as well as the food for authenticity

Alternatives:
-------------
For now, placeholders will show as dark rectangles with labels. Once real images are
added, they will appear in place of the placeholders automatically (the HTML just
uses standard <img> with the src being the filename).
